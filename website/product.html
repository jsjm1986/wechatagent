One console for the entire private-domain loop

后台按「运营 / 知识 / 系统」三组组织全部 18 个频道。从单人长期运营，到知识沉淀、AI 总控、请示裁决、自我演化与成效复盘——每个频道都对应真实可用的后端能力与可审计的数据。

The console organizes all 18 channels into three groups — Operations, Knowledge, System. From per-person long-term operations to knowledge curation, AI command, escalation, self-evolution and outcome review — every channel maps to real backend capability and auditable data.

Wiki 管理

Knowledge Wiki

Knowledge Wiki

管理知识库领域 schema、缺口信号与切片修订历史。9 类知识切片互相编织成知识仓；每次修订双写——不可变修订流 + 可变当前切片，带 sha256 校验。AI 永不自动核验，新知识一律落草稿待审。

Manage domain schema, gap signals and revision history. 9 wiki types weave into a linked store; each revision double-writes — an immutable revision stream plus the mutable current slice, sha256-checked. The AI never self-verifies; new knowledge lands as draft for review.
